Cold vs hot. A miss costs the full raster — 2,300–3,900 ns, which is where "parity with stb" comes from. A hit costs 12–23 ns. Decode falls as size grows because flattening subdivides curves less at coarser scales, not because less work is done.

Reading it honestly

  • Caching: the real win. 110–320× cheaper per glyph on a hit. This is the claim that holds.
  • Raster speed vs stb doing identical work: parity (0.85–1.03×). Not a win, and never was the goal.
  • Zero flushes across 18,800 hits — arenaFor sizing plus one-victim recycling, on a realistic Latin working set.
  • The bench cannot see the by-pointer change from 0.5.0→0.5.1; that needs the per-glyph microbench, since Lookup is ~1% of a glyph.
